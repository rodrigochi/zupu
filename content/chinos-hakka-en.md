## Who are the Hakka?

The Hakka (客家, *Kèjiā*, "guest families") are a Chinese ethnic group with their own identity, dialect and culture. Unlike other Chinese groups, the Hakka are defined primarily by their history of successive migrations within China and around the world.

Our family — the 徐 (Xú) clan of Niánkēng — is Hakka.

## The Hakka dialect

Hakka, or *kèjiāhuà* (客家話), is a Sinitic language clearly distinct from Cantonese and Mandarin. It preserves features of Middle Chinese that other dialects have lost. It is the language our ancestors spoke in Niánkēng and Héshān — and the reason our surname is *Chi* rather than *Xu* in Chile.
