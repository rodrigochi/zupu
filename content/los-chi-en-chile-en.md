## From 徐 to Chi

The Chinese surname 徐 (*Xú* in Mandarin) arrived in Chile as *Chi*, a romanisation based on the Hakka pronunciation of the character. This small shift — from 徐 to Chi — encapsulates in itself the story of our family's adaptation in Chile.
